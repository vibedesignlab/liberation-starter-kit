# Quality rubric

Audit the completed case independently. Score each criterion, cite concrete locations, and fail critical violations regardless of total score.

## Scoring

### Evidence sufficiency — 20

- 0–5: major dimensions missing; corpus is narrow, duplicate-heavy, or poorly sourced.
- 6–12: basic coverage with material gaps or source-tier imbalance.
- 13–17: representative primary and secondary evidence with disclosed limitations.
- 18–20: strong cross-channel, temporal, verbal, and visual coverage appropriate to scope.

### Traceability and epistemic clarity — 20

- 0–5: claims lack evidence or fact, inference, and invention are mixed.
- 6–12: partial citation and inconsistent labels.
- 13–17: major claims are traceable with confidence and scope limits.
- 18–20: evidence chains, alternatives, exceptions, and uncertainty are consistently explicit.

### Grammar fidelity — 25

- 0–6: output copies surface style or relies on generic adjectives.
- 7–15: some useful rules but weak causal explanation or cross-channel consistency.
- 16–21: executable grammar kernel grounded in repeated patterns.
- 22–25: distinctive causal logic remains coherent across strategy, language, visuals, behavior, and imagery.

### Target-product fit — 20

- 0–5: noun-swapped source system; category, audience, or usability ignored.
- 6–12: plausible translation with unresolved product conflicts.
- 13–17: target truth and constraints reshape the source grammar intelligently.
- 18–20: the new system feels native, usable, differentiated, and consistently generative.

### Originality and imitation distance — 15

- 0–3: protected or proprietary expressions are reproduced.
- 4–8: recognizable imitation remains in several key assets or compositions.
- 9–12: grammar is transferred with mostly distinct expressions.
- 13–15: clear conceptual lineage coexists with strong independent identity.

## Critical failures

Fail the dossier when any item applies:

- A core rule has no evidence and is not labeled as a hypothesis.
- `Observed`, `Inferred`, and `Transferred` content is materially conflated.
- A logo, slogan, proprietary name, unique type treatment, trade dress, or signature composition is reproduced.
- Image direction is not grounded in a registered visual corpus.
- Exact token values are presented as final without product validation.
- A required positioning, verbal, visual, token, image, mapping, or evidence section is absent.
- The dossier hides critical source gaps or contradictory evidence.

## Pass conditions

- Score at least 80/100.
- Leave no critical failure unresolved.
- Score at least half of the available points in every category.
- Provide a prioritized correction for every deduction of three or more points.

## Audit output

Return:

1. Verdict: `pass`, `pass-with-revisions`, or `fail`.
2. Score table with rationale.
3. Critical failures.
4. Blockers.
5. High-impact revisions.
6. Evidence and corpus gaps.
7. Copy-risk findings.
8. Strongest parts worth preserving.
9. Re-audit checklist.

Do not rewrite the dossier during an audit unless explicitly asked. Do not award points for polish that hides weak evidence.

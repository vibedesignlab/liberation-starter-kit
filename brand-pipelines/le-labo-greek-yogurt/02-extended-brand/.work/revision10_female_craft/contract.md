# Revision 10 — Female Craft Stewardship

## Locked direction

- MORA is a premium craft Greek-yogurt brand led by a symbolic female maker: the person who selects ingredients, remembers differences, controls transformation, and takes responsibility for the last condition before serving.
- Mother, wife, and keeper of the kitchen are symbolic sources of culinary responsibility, not consumer stereotypes, prescribed gender roles, or decorative femininity.
- Retain Le Labo's source jobs: observable labor proves craft; working materials and aged functional space create premium tension; information-bearing objects become graphics; controlled records create individuality; one human action activates a repeated family.
- Do not copy the Le Labo wordmark, ingredient-plus-number naming, exact pharmacy label grid, perfume silhouette, city/date ritual, manifesto cadence, trade dress, or Le Journal format.
- Replace the bright immaculate ivory atelier with a dark graphite food manufactory: brick/concrete shell, sealed hygienic food-contact surfaces, stainless work islands, functional inventory, low-key task light, and visible use without dirt or food-safety theater.
- Product architecture is a low, wide, transparent wide-mouth spoon jar. At least 60–70% of the edible content remains visible. A partial uncoated paper batch record carries fixed food-native specifications plus limited genuine maker check marks; mandatory legal data stays standardized.
- The product family remains six directional recipes: Thyme Honey, Fig Leaf, Roasted Buckwheat, Citrus Peel, Black Sesame, Olive Oil & Sea Salt.
- Intermediate states are target-native proof: cultured gel, acid whey, strained base, aqueous infusion, syrup, grain slurry, seed paste, oil phase or validated emulsion, folded batch.
- Key visual is photography-led and process-based: one input, one tool, one action, one intermediate output, one record. Mood may be dark; factual product proof remains color-accurate and legible.
- Founder biography is not invented. The female-founder story remains a directional causal spine until name, role, family context, origin event, first experiment, records, quotations, and portrait rights are confirmed.

## Shard interface

Each branch writes only its assigned JSON file in this directory. Do not edit canonical S2 files, registries, generated stories, S3, or shared project code.

Every shard must be valid JSON and include:

- `revision_id`: `revision10_female_craft`
- `owned_sections`: exact full replacement objects for the assigned Stage 2 section keys
- `transfer_input_additions`: concise input decisions relevant to the branch
- `boundary_additions`: assumptions, factual limits, and protected-source boundaries introduced by the branch
- `handoff_notes`: unresolved risks and integration notes

Root is the sole canonical integrator and final validator.

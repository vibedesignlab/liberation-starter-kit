# Revision 11 Fast Mood Contract

Purpose: five fast mood images, one direct representative image for each numbered user feedback item. These are review candidates, not approved S2 anchors or production evidence.

## Shared series lock

- 3:2 landscape, photorealistic commercial/documentary food photography.
- Dark graphite shadows, aged brick and sealed concrete on non-food-contact surfaces, clean brushed stainless on food-contact surfaces.
- Cultured-milk white, translucent acid whey, uncoated paper, steel and actual ingredient color remain legible in the dark room.
- Female authority is expressed through selecting, comparing, stopping, arranging and recording—not decorative femininity.
- The vessel is a transparent low-wide 150 g wide-mouth spoon jar, never a perfume bottle.
- Partial paper Batch Record is food-native and restrained; avoid detailed generated microtext.
- No Le Labo wordmark, ingredient-plus-number naming, exact pharmacy-label grid, city/date ritual, perfume tools or trade dress.
- No pink/floral styling, home-kitchen cliché, smiling lifestyle pose, dirty food-contact surface, theatrical lab glassware, CGI food swirl or crushed blacks.
- Exactly one built-in image-generation call per requested image. Basic visual/file check only; no second-pass correction unless the user later requests it.

## Feedback mapping

1. Rough craft and curation — an unpolished working table where a woman maker selects and compares raw materials and intermediate states inside the dark manufactory.
2. Rebuilt brand mood and package — dark brick/concrete food manufactory plus wide-mouth transparent jar, visible food and partial Batch Record.
3. Storytelling devices — flagship work counter where a woman maker attaches a personal Batch Record beside a visible archive of intermediate outputs.
4. Scientific and artisanal process — one evidence-rich process table showing culturing, separation, infusion, folding and recording through real food-native states and tools.
5. Key visual — ambient material photography of pure ingredients and MORA's transformation states, centered on strained yogurt, acid whey, thyme infusion and honey fold.

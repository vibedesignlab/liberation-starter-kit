# Revision 16 — Three-frame Analog Editorial Story Contract

## Outcome

Reduce the current six-frame brand-mood production series to three necessary narrative frames and make them read as one analog magazine story photographed on the same day in the same compact MORA store-workshop.

1. **The Selection / beginning** — ingredients are received, compared and excluded.
2. **The Process / middle** — cultured base is strained, separated and folded through a readable work sequence.
3. **The Last Measure / ending** — the same maker stops, judges and writes the final record.

Product still life, personal archive portrait and material macro leave the current main brand-mood production series. They may remain as historical/supporting assets but are not equal narrative chapters.

## Series lock

- Same adult female maker identity, black functional workwear, hair, natural skin and non-performative concentration.
- Same lived-in urban storefront-to-workshop location: repaired plaster, irregular utility tile, old threshold, dark timber, blackened non-contact steel, clean stainless food-contact zones.
- Same daytime window source from the street side plus localized neutral-warm task light. No full-room studio fill.
- 3:2 landscape. Environment frames use a normal documentary perspective. Keep front scenes architecturally front-facing and the process scene strict side-facing.
- Same human/object scale, food states, record semantics and operational inventory.
- Same analog capture/print response across all three frames.

## Analog magazine capture and print response

- Photorealistic 35 mm high-speed color-negative documentary capture, visually equivalent to an ISO 800 small-frame negative scanned for an independent food/culture magazine.
- Grain must be clearly visible at Storybook display size and original size, especially across flat repaired plaster, midtone skin, black workwear and deep-but-open shadows.
- Grain is irregular, organic and luminance-led: larger in shadows/midtones, finer in bright food and window highlights. Never an even monochrome digital-noise overlay.
- Slightly lifted black floor, soft highlight shoulder and modest color-density variation. Keep yogurt, whey, herbs, honey and paper factually legible.
- Localized gentle halation/bloom only around the bright window edge and practical lamp. No global haze or neon-red halo.
- Mild lens/scan softness, tiny focus falloff toward frame edges, natural micro-contrast and occasional minute scan texture. Hands, food contact and the decision action stay readable.
- Restrained warm task-light highlights against cool available daylight; no teal-orange grade, sepia nostalgia or bleached fashion look.
- Retouching stays editorial: retain pores, fine lines, under-eye texture, fabric creases, stainless micro-scratches and small architectural repair marks.

## Narrative behavior

- Each frame must show one decisive action, not a posed model or arranged tableau.
- The maker looks at the material or record, never at camera.
- Keep imperfect real-world overlaps and partial foreground occlusion where already present.
- The three frames must read without captions as cause → transformation → decision.

## Preserve

- The exact R15 subject identity, hands, action, product/material state, object scale, room geometry, camera axis and copy-safe architecture of each edit target.
- No new people, ingredients, labels, tools, furniture, brand copy or invented production data.
- No change to the partial Batch Record meaning or jar geometry.

## Avoid

- Uniform procedural grain, fake film border, sprocket holes, date stamp, light leak, dust storm, scratched-film damage or heavy vignette.
- HDR clarity, razor-sharp total frame, perfect symmetry, cloned inventory, glossy CGI reflections, waxy skin, repeated pore texture, plastic food, crushed blacks or clipped window white.
- Cinematic fog, stage spotlight, fashion pose, farmhouse nostalgia, perfume/apothecary props or exact Le Labo trade dress.

## Quick done check

- Three files only in the current production series.
- Grain is visually present but does not obscure hands, food states or paper.
- Same color/contrast/grain family across all three.
- Selection → Process → Last Measure is immediately legible.
- Each branch saves one PNG and one matching prompt Markdown under its exclusive R16 filename.

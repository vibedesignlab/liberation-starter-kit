# Revision 17 — Raw Ingredient UI Series Contract

## Role

Six color-faithful UI ingredient-proof cards, one for each MORA recipe. These are not brand-mood frames and do not replace the three-frame R16 story.

## Shared lock

- Image 1 is always `REF-07 — UI Background Master` and is the sole background, horizon, camera, ground-plane, exposure and reflection authority.
- 3:2 landscape, mathematically strict front elevation, neutral eye-level product-camera stance, 70–90 mm visual behavior.
- Keep every ingredient and vessel fully visible, centered as one compact group on the same baseline, occupying 18–24% of frame height.
- Use ordinary low food-prep dishes or cups. Ingredients stay at believable culinary scale; no oversized leaves, seeds, peel, salt or vessels.
- Show raw recipe inputs only. No yogurt, finished product, infusion, syrup, slurry, paste, emulsion, package, label, paper or generated text.
- Color-faithful 4800 K high-CRI task-light response. Keep ingredient color factual and highlights unclipped.
- Connect to R16 through restrained physical capture only: fine irregular 35 mm color-negative grain visible in empty plaster and stainless areas, lifted open blacks and a soft highlight shoulder. Grain stays finer over food and never changes ingredient color.
- Natural asymmetry, plausible vessel thickness, grounded contact shadows, consistent reflections and real ingredient surface variation.

## Avoid

Styled abundance, scattered garnish, rustic sacks or boards, botanical fantasy, honey dipper or honeycomb, whole fruit, luxury-black styling, laboratory glassware, beakers, perfume cues, CGI gloss, HDR, total-frame sharpness, uniform digital noise, film borders, sprockets, date stamps, light leaks and fake damage.

## Frame order

1. Thyme & Honey — selected thyme leaves and natural amber honey in two ordinary low vessels.
2. Fig Leaf — accepted and rejected fig-leaf material visibly separated in two low trays.
3. Roasted Buckwheat — one restrained measured lot of actual roasted buckwheat groats.
4. Citrus Peel — selected fine peel and rejected white pith or damaged peel visibly separated.
5. Black Sesame — one restrained measured lot of actual black sesame seed.
6. Olive Oil & Sea Salt — natural olive oil and actual-scale sea salt in two ordinary low vessels.

## Targeted final revision prompt (exact)

The initial result preserved the field and microtexture but risked reading as a scraped furrow with honey resting on the surface. The following one-change correction produced the final asset.

```text
Targeted revision of the immediately previous Infusion Bloom image.

Preserve exactly:
- 3:2 landscape framing
- continuous warm mineral-ivory dense yogurt field
- broad quiet negative space
- restrained off-center lower-right amber-green region
- authentic yogurt, honey, and thyme microtexture
- soft directional light, matte low-contrast finish
- no objects, people, product, text, logo, whole herbs, bounded photograph, horizon, or tabletop

Change only the material behavior of the amber-green region:
- remove the impression of a scraped furrow, torn channel, crater, exposed cavity, or spoon track
- remove distinct honey droplets or honey sitting visibly on top
- make the amber presence read as a shallow translucent layer blooming from beneath an unbroken yogurt surface, with gentle capillary diffusion and soft irregular tonal density
- keep only sparse tiny fragmented thyme micro-fibers suspended at mixed shallow depths, never a whole leaf or sprig
- let the amber-green texture emerge seamlessly and dissolve softly into ivory on all sides, with no hard edge
- keep it restrained, asymmetrical, low contrast, and physically believable as controlled infusion
- do not create swirls, marbling, splash, pour, smoke, glow, gradient mesh, CGI jelly, or a decorative graphic motif

The final image should feel like a minimal matte material field with photographic ingredient truth, not a close-up of a damaged yogurt surface. No text or watermark.
```

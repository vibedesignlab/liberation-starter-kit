- Execution: built-in image generation

## Prompt

Create “Persistent Trace,” a minimal background-integrated material field that visualizes layered flavor and a long clean finish. A dense warm mineral-ivory Greek-yogurt field fills the entire 3:2 landscape canvas. One single broad furrow crosses the field and leaves three causally related traces: one close sharp honey-amber ribbon with a few tiny thyme-green micro-fibers, one translucent middle trace embedded below the surface, and one very faint deep trace that continues and dissolves into broad negative space. The background itself is the material; there is no framed photograph and no separate tabletop scene.

Style/medium: hybrid image using physically believable photographic yogurt, honey-viscosity and herb-fiber microtexture integrated into a restrained matte graphic field; quiet editorial abstraction, not 100% photography and not generic 3D render.

Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape, full bleed, about 60 percent quiet negative space, the trace begins with tactile definition in the right-center and thins as it travels toward the left, no hard cutout edges, material textures emerge from and dissolve back into the same background.

Lighting/mood: one soft directional source, preserved midtones and shadow detail, calm precise tension, no dramatic rays, no crushed blacks.

Color palette: warm mineral ivory, soft graphite micro-shadow, natural honey amber and restrained thyme green only; ingredient color never becomes the full background.

Materials/textures: convincing dense yogurt body, small thickness variations, one broad fold/furrow, sticky translucent honey ribbon, sparse real thyme micro-fibers, natural asymmetry, no uniform CGI gloss.

Constraints: no people, no package, no vessel, no spoon, no typography, no logo, no label, no watermark; no bounded photo rectangle; no floating whole herbs or ingredients; no particle explosion; no liquid splash; no perfume smoke or scent trail; no decorative gradient mesh; no 3D jelly blobs; no marble swirl or fully mixed marbling; no laboratory equipment; no rustic styling. The image is a directional concept visual and must not imply a validated recipe or finished food claim.

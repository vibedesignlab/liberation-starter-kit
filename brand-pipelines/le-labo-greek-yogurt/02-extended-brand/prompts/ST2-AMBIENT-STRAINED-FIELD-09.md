- Series: `The Background Is the Batch`

- Execution: built-in image generation
- Final selection: one targeted built-in refinement of the initial generation

## Initial generation prompt (exact)

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: MORA Stage 2 ambient material-system key visual, full-bleed web and Storybook background, 3:2 landscape
Primary request: Create “Strained Field,” an abstract yet materially truthful image of yogurt straining. The entire frame is one continuous warm mineral-ivory yogurt material field. Within the field, authentic fine cloth-fiber microtexture gradually emerges as if embedded beneath and inside the yogurt rather than laid on top. Across the lower third, a very thin translucent whey layer visibly separates from the dense ivory mass and drains gently downward under gravity. Above that separation, the remaining yogurt field looks subtly denser, more opaque, and softly compressed. Make the separation operation physically legible but minimal and ambient.
Scene/backdrop: No scene and no tabletop; the background itself is the batch. Full-bleed continuous field with broad quiet negative space, especially through the upper-left and center. Material details emerge from and dissolve back into the same background with no cutout or rectangular photographic edge.
Subject: Warm mineral-ivory cultured-yogurt density, real woven straining-cloth fibers, and a narrow translucent whey membrane; no discrete objects.
Style/medium: Restrained hybrid of photographic food-and-fiber macro texture and a minimal matte graphic field. Editorially refined, tactile, physically plausible, naturally asymmetric, not a conventional photograph and not CGI.
Composition/framing: Exact 3:2 landscape. One broad calm field. The denser yogurt occupies most of the frame; cloth fibers become gently legible within a soft diagonal-to-horizontal pressure region; the whey membrane forms a delicate descending separation in the lower third with slight uneven thickness. No central hero object.
Lighting/mood: One soft directional source, quiet diffused atelier light, preserved midtones and fine shadow detail, contemplative and precise; no dramatic rays, glow, or crushed blacks.
Color palette: Mineral ivory and warm milk white with only faint soft-graphite fiber shadows and very subtle pale whey translucency. Do not introduce amber or green in this separation frame.
Materials/textures: Actual strained-yogurt thickness, minute irregular curd tension, truthful woven cloth fibers partially submerged, wet translucency at the whey boundary, tiny natural thickness variations, matte rather than glossy.
Constraints: Material textures must appear integrated within the background and fade into it without edges. The downward drainage and denser retained field must be clearly readable on close inspection. No text, logo, label, watermark, people, hands, product, package, vessel, utensil, laboratory equipment, or identifiable room.
Avoid: bounded or rectangular photo, conventional tabletop still life, visible cloth sheet as a separate object, floating ingredients, herbs, honey, particle explosion, liquid splash, perfume smoke, scent trail, decorative gradient mesh, 3D jelly blob, marble swirl, fully mixed marbling, rustic craft styling, ingredient-colored background, uniform CGI gloss, hard graphic dividing line.
```

## Targeted refinement prompt used for the final image (exact)

```text
Targeted refinement of the immediately preceding “Strained Field” image. Preserve the exact 3:2 landscape framing, warm mineral-ivory palette, quiet negative space, soft directional light, photographic food-and-fiber microtexture, and the concept of yogurt separation.

Change only the material integration: remove the appearance of a separate visible gauze sheet and remove every hanging drip or syrup-like strand. Turn the whole frame into one continuous matte yogurt material field. Let the cloth weave be only a faint embedded microtexture that rises within the ivory density and then dissolves away without an object edge. Represent whey as one very thin translucent subsurface membrane with a gentle downward tonal migration across the lower third—not a pool, waterfall, or droplets. Make the upper retained field subtly denser and more opaque so separation remains legible through density and translucency rather than literal objects.

No hard horizon, no separate cloth object, no liquid drips, no photo rectangle, no tabletop, no vessel, no tools, no product, no people, no text, logo, label, or watermark. Keep natural asymmetry, preserved midtones, matte food texture, and a broad calm full-bleed ambient field; avoid CGI gloss, gradient mesh, jelly forms, splash advertising, smoke, marbling, or rustic styling.
```

## Evidence boundary

This is a directional ambient concept visual. It explains separation and density change; it is not evidence of a production recipe, SOP, sensory panel, or finished food appearance.

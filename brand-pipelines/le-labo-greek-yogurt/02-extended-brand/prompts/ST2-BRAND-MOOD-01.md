# ST2-BRAND-MOOD-01 — Ingredient infusion process

## Decision package

- Web role: brand-mood editorial image for the provenance/process section.
- Information goal: turn premium craft into one observable food-making action and show natural material entering dense yogurt.
- Viewpoint: natural eye-level medium-wide documentary frame.
- Physical execution: real contemporary food atelier, one maker, one infusion action, soft daylight, food-safe tools and believable ingredient behavior.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: horizontal brand-mood editorial photograph for a premium Greek-yogurt landing page.
Purpose: show that MORA’s value comes from a careful natural-ingredient infusion step, without borrowing perfume-lab imagery or turning the maker into a posed model.

Scene: a contemporary daylight food atelier with a pale limewashed wall, one light oak shelf, a clean stainless work surface, folded undyed linen, a ceramic bowl, and a small amount of fresh thyme. The room is functional and modern, not rustic nostalgia and not a laboratory. Keep props sparse and each object visibly useful.

Subject and action: one real food maker in a simple charcoal cotton apron stands naturally at the work surface, framed from upper torso through both hands. The maker looks down at the task. In one clear action, one hand slowly pours a thin amber thyme-and-honey infusion from a small clear glass pitcher into a wide bowl of very dense white strained yogurt while the other hand steadies the bowl. The stream, pitcher, bowl, wrists, and yogurt surface must connect with believable scale, gravity, contact, and occlusion. A broad stainless spatula rests on the work surface but is not being used. No packaged product in this frame.

Photography: photorealistic observational food-process photography, natural wide-normal 35 mm perspective, medium-wide eye-level frame, gentle spatial layering. Large north-window daylight from frame left creates one coherent soft light direction and gradual shadows; restrained warm-neutral color, retained midtone detail, slight natural grain, invisible retouching. Focus favors hands, stream, and yogurt while the maker and room remain legible. Preserve real skin variation, cloth weave, steel brushing, glass refraction, yogurt ridges, and irregular thyme leaves.

Physical constraints: the infusion stream must enter the bowl continuously; dense yogurt should hold soft folds and a shallow amber trail; the glass pitcher must refract the visible room plausibly; all objects share one camera height and shadow direction; hands have correct anatomy and natural grip. The image should feel quiet, exact, edible, and contemporary.

Avoid: no Le Labo references, no pharmacy or perfumery lab, no beakers, pipettes, medicine bottles, specimen labels, black industrial cabinetry, brick nostalgia, vintage 1980s kitchen styling, posed smiling model, chef costume, excessive ingredients, fake steam, splashes, floating utensils, plastic skin, procedural food texture, crushed blacks, clipped yogurt highlights, CGI cleanliness, text, logo, watermark.

## Observable checks

- One person performs one readable infusion action.
- The setting is a modern working food space, not a themed lab or rustic set.
- Ingredient stream, glass, hands, bowl, and shadows are physically consistent.
- Yogurt texture and thyme-honey trail look edible and specific.
- No brand-copy imitation or fragrance cue appears.

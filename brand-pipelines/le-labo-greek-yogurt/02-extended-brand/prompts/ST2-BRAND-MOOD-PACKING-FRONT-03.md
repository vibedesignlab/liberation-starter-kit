# ST2-BRAND-MOOD-PACKING-FRONT-03 — The Packing Table, frontal

## Decision package

- Web role: registered brand-mood anchor for the craft and packaging transition.
- Information goal: prove that packaging is the final observable craft action: a sealed food-safe cup receives a dry factual fiber collar by hand.
- Viewpoint: strict 100% frontal, straight-on and eye-level.
- Series lock: same maker, exact action phase, worktable, compact cup sealer, cup, foil, collar, light direction and material palette as the side-profile companion.
- Dramatic device: hard-but-broad raking daylight across fiber, metal, translucent cup and hands—not nostalgic darkness or theatrical props.

## Final generation prompt

Image 1 is the identity, wardrobe and workspace continuity reference. Image 2 is the authoritative packaging-material reference. Recompose a new photograph; do not merely overlay objects. Preserve the same adult female maker identity, tied-back dark hair, charcoal cotton shirt and apron, and contemporary food atelier. Use the package geometry and material sequence from Image 2.

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: horizontal brand-mood editorial photograph for a premium Greek-yogurt landing page.
Purpose: make the final packing action and the roles of food, metal, fiber and hand immediately understandable.

Scene/backdrop: minimal contemporary food-production atelier with a cool mineral plaster wall and a clean brushed stainless packing table. A compact modern stainless tabletop cup-sealing press sits clearly at frame right, idle after sealing, with no fake controls and no industrial clutter. On the table are only one sealed smoke-clear low-wide yogurt cup, one separate dry pressed-fiber collar, one matte aluminum foil disk, and one stainless check scale in the far background. No kitchen shelf styling, ceramics display, wood, decorative jars or vintage furniture.

Strict camera geometry: exact 100% frontal, eye level, sensor plane parallel to the back wall and front edge of the worktable. Wall, table edge and sealing press are square to the image plane. No diagonal convergence and no three-quarter camera position. The maker stands centered with shoulders equally visible and torso facing directly forward.

Subject/action: the same maker looks down at her hands. Both hands are at the center of the frame, sliding and locking a separate pale mineral-gray pressed-fiber collar around only the lower third of an already foil-sealed warm smoke-clear yogurt cup. Freeze the exact moment when one hand holds the cup steady and the other closes the collar's small tab. The matte aluminum pull-tab seal remains fully visible above the collar. A narrow square-cut aperture in the collar reveals the real white yogurt and amber thyme-honey trace through the translucent primary vessel. The collar is visibly dry and never touches the food or seal. No readable text is required in this mood frame.

Composition: disciplined frontal symmetry with the hands, cup and collar in the central 35% as the sole action. The maker occupies the center; the idle cup sealer on the right and one unused flat collar on the left form functional context without competing. Generous clear wall surrounds the silhouette. The sequence must read in one glance: sealed cup first, dry collar second.

Lighting/mood: one hard-but-broad daylight source from frame left rakes across the scene, producing a bright plane on the maker's hands, foil edge, translucent rim, pressed fiber and brushed steel. Long controlled shadows fall rightward but retain detail. Natural skin, cool mineral neutrals, charcoal cloth, white yogurt, honest amber food trace; no sepia, beige wash, colored gel or underexposure.

Photography/realism: natural 45–50 mm perspective, realistic editorial process photography, subtle grain and invisible retouching. Prioritize believable fingers and grip, independent material thickness, foil tension, cup wall, collar seam, tab engagement, table contact, food visibility and one coherent light direction.

Avoid: paper cup, paper lid, fiber touching yogurt, unsealed open product being sleeved, rustic kraft, torn deckle paper, farmhouse craft, apothecary or perfume trade dress, label grid, amber bottle, beakers, pipettes, droppers, test tubes, chemistry theatre, vintage machine, black industrial cabinetry, dark moody perfume campaign, smiling pose, looking at camera, extra people, alternate outfit, diagonal camera, three-quarter pose, text, logo, watermark, distorted hands, floating cup, impossible shadows, CGI cleanliness.

## Observable checks

- Camera is measurably front-on; shoulders, table and wall are square.
- The cup is visibly sealed before the dry collar is applied.
- The collar is a separate lower-third layer with a real seam, tab and aperture.
- The role of each material is legible without caption.
- The scene looks like current small-batch food packing, not a kitchen, pharmacy, perfume lab or rustic workshop.

## Final generation prompt

Use Image 1 only as the identity, wardrobe, workspace, utensil, ingredient and material-continuity reference. Recompose the scene into a new strict side-profile camera position.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Horizontal supporting brand-mood editorial photograph for a premium infused Greek-yogurt landing page. The scene is the same minimal contemporary daylight food atelier: pale limewashed wall, one light-oak shelf, clean stainless worktop, sparse food-safe tools, no decorative clutter. It must feel current, exact and edible—not nostalgic, rustic, pharmaceutical, perfumery-like or laboratory-themed.

Strict camera geometry: exact 90-degree right-side profile, perfectly level and eye height. The camera lens axis is perpendicular to the maker's sagittal plane. Show only the maker's right facial profile: one eye contour, one nose profile and one cheek line. The torso, shoulders, forearms, pitcher, liquid stream and bowl occupy a single clear lateral plane. The maker does not rotate toward the camera. No frontal face, no three-quarter face and no over-the-shoulder view. Keep the visible worktop edge straight and level rather than diagonally receding.

Subject and action: the same real adult female food maker from Image 1, with the same tied-back hair and charcoal cotton shirt/apron, looks down along her profile toward the work. In one continuous action, her nearer hand tilts the same small clear glass pitcher and pours a thin amber thyme-honey infusion into the same wide ceramic bowl of very dense white strained yogurt while the other hand steadies the bowl. Arrange pitcher, stream, bowl and both hands so their physical connection is fully readable in profile. The stream must visibly enter the yogurt; the yogurt holds dense folds and a shallow amber trace. No packaged product and no text.

Composition: place the maker in one half of the frame and allow generous forward negative space in the direction of her hands and gaze. Preserve the same subject size and camera height as the frontal companion. Use the maker's profile, forearms and liquid stream as one precise lateral silhouette. The scene feels dramatic because the same hard-but-broad daylight from frame left draws a bright plane across the profile and food action, leaving long controlled shadows across steel and wall. Preserve readable shadow detail, natural skin tone and white-yogurt highlight texture—no crushed blacks and no clipped whites.

Photography and physical realism: realistic editorial food-process photography, natural 40–50 mm perspective, no ultra-wide distortion, subtle photographic grain and invisible retouching. Prioritize believable profile anatomy, hands and grip, glass thickness and refraction, liquid continuity, gravity, bowl contact, yogurt viscosity, steel brushing, cloth weave and one coherent light/shadow direction. The result should feel restrained, modern and cinematic without resembling a fashion pose or CGI render.

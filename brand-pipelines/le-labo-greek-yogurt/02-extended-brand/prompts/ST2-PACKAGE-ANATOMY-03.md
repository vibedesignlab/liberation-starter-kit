# ST2-PACKAGE-ANATOMY-03 — Material Witness anatomy

## Decision package

- Web role: detail proof / packaging explainer.
- Information goal: make the package layer order and each material's job unmistakable without using a technical diagram or text labels.
- Viewpoint: elevated oblique still life with all components separated on one ground plane.
- Evidence boundary: material and construction direction only; exact resin, barrier, foil specification, paper stock, adhesives, print method and recyclability remain supplier and food-safety validation gates.

## Final generation prompt

Image 1 is the authoritative package geometry and material reference. Create a new exploded still-life photograph that separates the package into real components without changing its form or materials.

Use case: product-mockup and detail-proof photography.
Asset type: horizontal package-anatomy still life for a premium Greek-yogurt brand report.
Purpose: show what touches food, what seals it, what carries facts, and what groups the product.

Scene/backdrop: cool graphite-gray stone or mineral surface, no wall horizon, no decorative kitchen scene. Arrange four package components in one precise left-to-right sequence with generous spacing and no overlaps: (1) one open warm smoke-clear low-wide primary cup holding dense white strained yogurt with an amber thyme-honey trace, (2) one separate thin matte aluminum peel seal with a plain pull tab, (3) one unassembled pale mineral-gray pressed-fiber collar opened flat so its fibers, crisp edges, fold seam, small locking tab and narrow square-cut aperture are visible, and (4) one shallow molded-fiber two-cup carrier with compressed ribs and precise corners. Add one brushed stainless dessert spoon only as a familiar food-use cue.

The primary cup remains unprinted. The food supplies white, amber and thyme-green color. The foil supplies a cool metal tone. The collar and carrier show honest compressed fiber texture with hard rectilinear cuts, not distressed rustic paper. On the flat collar, allow only exact black word “MORA” once in a restrained modern sans serif; no other readable text.

Composition/framing: elevated 30-degree oblique camera, natural 55–70 mm perspective with minimal distortion. Keep all components complete, aligned on a shared baseline, and within one focal plane. Leave clear negative space between layers so the physical sequence is obvious.

Lighting/mood: hard-but-broad raking daylight from upper left reveals translucent wall thickness, yogurt relief, foil edge and fiber compression. Controlled short shadows map every component to the same ground plane. Cool-neutral color response with honest food color; high material separation; no beige monochrome.

Physical realism: believable food viscosity, thin cup wall and rounded food-safe rim, foil thickness and pull tab, independent collar thickness and crease memory, functional tab and aperture, credible pressed-fiber carrier ribs. Nothing floats and no layer is fused to another.

Avoid: infographic labels, arrows, numbers, exploded 3D CGI, paper cup, paper seal, fiber contacting food, clear perfume bottle, apothecary packaging, adhesive pharmacy label, amber glass, dropper, beaker, pipette, rustic kraft, deckle edges, twine, wood, linen, pottery, vintage props, glossy luxury, decorative color swatches, excess garnish, extra text, watermark.

## Observable checks

- The food-contact cup, metal seal, dry information collar and carrier are four separate physical objects.
- Material roles can be inferred from the construction alone.
- Fiber reads dense and modern through compression and precision, not rustic through distress.
- Food is the only source of ingredient color.
- The image cannot be mistaken for perfume, skincare, candle or supplement packaging.

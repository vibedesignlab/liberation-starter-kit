# ST2-PROCESS-FINISH-04 — The Finish Counter

## Communication job

Translate personalization into a controlled immediate-serving ritual: the recipe stays fixed while the guest chooses one approved visual finish for a single cup.

## Generation prompt

## Acceptance checks

- One finished edible cup is unmistakably the outcome.
- Three samples communicate degrees of the same approved finish, not custom ingredients.
- The maker applies a measured food-safe finish and the sealed retail formula is not implied to change.
- The setting reads as a contemporary food atelier, not fragrance retail or bar service.

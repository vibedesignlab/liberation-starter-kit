# ST2-PROCESS-INFUSION-LIBRARY-02 — The Infusion Pantry

## Communication job

Show that MORA does not force every ingredient into one fake transparent “spirit.” Each ingredient becomes an honest, food-native intermediate with its own carrier, opacity and texture.

## Generation prompt

Create a new photorealistic premium food editorial still life in 3:2 landscape. Use the supplied MORA front photograph only as a material and environment reference: the same current minimal stainless-steel atelier, soft plaster wall, warm wood shelf, mineral ceramics, hard directional daylight, restrained ivory/charcoal/ingredient-color palette. No person is required.

Strict frontal camera, sensor parallel to the workbench and rear wall. Arrange exactly six distinct food-safe intermediate preparations in one disciplined horizontal library, each in a shallow ceramic bowl, wide-mouth glass food cup, or small stainless jug—not narrow bottles. Pair each vessel with one restrained raw ingredient cue so the relationship is instantly readable: (1) amber thyme-honey syrup with a thyme sprig, viscous and translucent; (2) very pale green-gold fig-leaf aqueous infusion with one clean fig leaf, fluid and lightly translucent; (3) roasted buckwheat tan toasted infusion/slurry with a few groats, softly opaque; (4) citrus-peel golden aqueous preparation with one peel curl, translucent; (5) black sesame charcoal fine slurry/paste with scattered black seeds, fully opaque; (6) olive-oil and sea-salt phase with a small golden oil pool and visible fine emulsion edge, translucent-to-cloudy. Maintain obvious differences in viscosity, opacity and vessel form. Include one fine mesh basket and one folded muslin filter at the far side as functional preparation evidence. Generous negative space, exact material rendering, no text.

No logo, label, writing, identical liquid colors, six matching bottles, whiskey tasting flight, shot glasses, perfume bottles, apothecary bottles, droppers, pipettes, chemistry glassware, cork stoppers, pharmaceuticals, dark speakeasy mood, rustic pantry, vintage kitchen, botanical clutter, fake smoke, floating ingredients, excessive garnish, or luxury gloss.

## Acceptance checks

- All six lineup ingredients are represented through visibly different intermediate states.
- Black sesame reads as paste/slurry and olive oil reads as an oil phase, not as identical clear spirits.
- Vessels and filters are current, food-safe and functional.
- The image reads as a modern preparation library, not whiskey, perfume or pharmacy.

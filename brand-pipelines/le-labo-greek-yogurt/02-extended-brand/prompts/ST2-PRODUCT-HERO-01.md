# ST2-PRODUCT-HERO-01 — MORA Thyme Honey

## Decision package

- Web role: landing-page product hero.
- Information goal: identify a 150 g single-serve Greek-yogurt cup, show its dense spoonable texture, and make the thyme-honey infusion trace legible at first glance.
- Viewpoint: eye-level three-quarter primary face; medium-wide frame.
- UI adaptation: product anchored in the right third; continuous low-detail copy-safe area across the left 45%; no shadow, prop, reflection, or contrast edge in that zone.
- Physical execution: restrained commercial food photograph; soft side daylight; full edible texture; plausible cup, lid, spoon, contact shadows, and print.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: wide landing-page product hero for a new premium Greek-yogurt brand.
Purpose: make the product form, single-serving use, thick texture, and natural thyme-honey infusion immediately understandable while preserving a clean headline and CTA zone on the left.

Scene: a warm cultured-cream seamless plaster surface and backdrop, quiet contemporary food studio, no kitchen clutter. Keep the entire left 45 percent as a continuous low-detail copy-safe field.

Subject: one open 150 g MORA Greek-yogurt cup anchored in the right third, shown from an eye-level three-quarter primary-face view. The vessel is low and wide, straight-sided, matte warm-ivory fiber-composite packaging with a softly rounded food-safe rim. The front has only two clean typographic lines, exact text “MORA” and “THYME HONEY”, in a modern wide sans serif; no other legible copy. A flat ivory peel lid leans behind the cup and carries one restrained irregular honey-gold brush trace with tiny thyme-green flecks; this trace is the flavor identifier, not decorative brand color. The open cup shows dense strained yogurt with natural spoon ridges and one irregular amber honey vein with sparse real thyme flecks folded through it. A small brushed stainless dessert spoon rests beside the cup with a believable shallow scoop of yogurt; one small thyme sprig only.

Photography: photorealistic premium food and packaging photography, medium-wide composition, natural 50 mm perspective, product occupies about 43 percent of frame height. Large diffused daylight from upper left wraps the matte cup and reveals yogurt relief; very soft fill keeps shadow detail. Cast shadow falls short and toward the back-right, fully outside the left copy-safe region. Neutral warm white balance, restrained contrast, gentle tonal rolloff, invisible retouching.

Physical constraints: dense yogurt must deform naturally around spoon marks; honey vein must sit within the yogurt rather than float above it; thyme flecks irregular and sparse. Cup and lid must have credible thickness and ground contact. All shadows share one light direction. Preserve highlight detail in yogurt and readable matte surface on the cup.

Avoid: no Le Labo references, no apothecary label, no pharmacy or laboratory styling, no perfume bottle, no glass jar, no numbered product name, no batch label, no black industrial fixtures, no vintage props, no excessive garnish, no dripping mess, no floating objects, no CGI gloss, no plastic-looking food, no cloned thyme leaves, no extra text, no watermark.

## Observable checks

- Product, lid, spoon, and shadows remain outside the left copy-safe field.
- “MORA” and “THYME HONEY” are the only visible label lines.
- The yogurt reads as dense and spoonable, not ice cream, cream, or liquid yogurt.
- Honey-gold appears only as the ingredient trace and flavor identifier.
- Cup geometry and contact shadow are physically plausible.

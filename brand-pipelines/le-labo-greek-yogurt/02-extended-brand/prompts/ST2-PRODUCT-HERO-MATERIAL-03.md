# ST2-PRODUCT-HERO-MATERIAL-03 — Material Witness hero

## Decision package

- Web role: landing-page product hero and first packaging-system proof.
- Information goal: identify a premium single-serve Greek-yogurt cup while making four things immediately legible: edible ingredient trace, sealed food-contact vessel, dry fiber information layer, and matte metal seal.
- Viewpoint: eye-level three-quarter primary face; medium-wide horizontal frame.
- UI adaptation: product group anchored in the right third; continuous low-detail copy-safe area across the left 45%.
- Material rule: paper carries facts; food carries color; tools and closures carry process proof.

## Packaging system lock

- Low, wide 150 g working-format cup; warm smoke-clear or softly translucent unprinted food-grade barrier vessel, pending supplier and cold-chain validation.
- Matte aluminum peel seal with a plain pull tab; never a paper lid.
- Removable dry pressed-fiber collar only around the lower third of the cup. The collar is dense, pale mineral-gray fiber with visible fibers, a precise fold seam, one square-cut vertical aperture, and one small locking tab.
- The aperture reveals the actual yogurt and ingredient trace through the primary vessel. No decorative color swatch or printed flavor stripe.
- The only visible front text is exact “MORA” and “THYME HONEY” in black restrained sans serif. No grid, batch theatre, dates, handwriting, pharmacy label, or ingredient-plus-number construction.

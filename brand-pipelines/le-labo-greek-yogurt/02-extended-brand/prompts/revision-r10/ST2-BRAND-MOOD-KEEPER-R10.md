# ST2-BRAND-MOOD-KEEPER-R10

## Role

Brand-mood anchor for a premium female-maker craft Greek yogurt manufactory. Photorealistic documentary commercial photography: one responsible maker, one decisive action and food-native process evidence.

## Generation prompt

Create a 3:2 landscape cinematic documentary photograph titled “The Keeper’s Last Measure”. Inside a dark working food manufactory, show one adult woman maker, approximately 38–52, absorbed in comparing two real process states and recording a final decision. She is not a lifestyle model, chef celebrity, mother caricature or laboratory scientist. She wears practical charcoal workwear with sleeves secured, hair tied back and clean food-safe preparation appropriate to the task. Natural skin and capable hands; no makeup emphasis, jewelry, apron nostalgia or direct eye contact with the camera.

Build the room from an aged exposed-brick and sealed-concrete non-contact shell with a spotless brushed-stainless food-contact island. The space should feel accumulated, functional and used, yet unequivocally hygienic. Use one directional task light and dim graphite ambient light. Keep deep shadows detailed rather than crushed; retain correct cultured-milk-white food color and stainless neutrality.

Show exactly one central decision: the maker compares a dense strained yogurt base with captured translucent acid whey, then places one genuine check mark on a small uncoated-paper Batch Record. Include one wide stainless bowl or straining vessel, one stainless paddle or spoon, the two food-native intermediate states, and the record. Her hand must either hover just before the mark or complete it; do not add unrelated gestures. The scene must visibly contain one input/state, one tool, one action, one intermediate output and one record.

Frame the stainless bench across the lower 40% of the image. Place the maker’s hands and the two compared states along the central visual axis. Reserve one clean 25% graphite negative-space zone on the left for landing-page copy; keep it free of tools, faces, bright edges and signage. Allow a restrained background archive of covered food-safe containers and pinned production records to suggest ongoing collection, but keep it secondary and softly resolved.

Camera and optics: full-frame environmental portrait, 40–50 mm equivalent lens, chest-to-bench framing, realistic human proportions, f/5.6–f/8 so hands, food states and record remain legible, subtle depth falloff into the room, low ISO texture, documentary micro-contrast, no fashion retouching, no orange-and-teal grade, no theatrical fog.

The emotional tone is quiet authority, responsibility and exacting care. The woman owns the decision because she selects, compares, stops and records—not because the room is styled as feminine.

## Composition lock

- 3:2 landscape.
- One woman only, not looking at camera.
- One decisive check/compare action only.
- Bench in lower 40%; hands and states on central axis.
- Left 25% continuous graphite copy-safe zone.
- Aged dark shell plus pristine stainless contact surfaces.
- Dense strained base and translucent acid whey both visible.
- One small Batch Record with a genuine variable mark.

## Avoid

Smiling lifestyle portrait, posed family scene, wife or mother cliché, young beauty-advertising model, pink, floral, lace, soft domestic styling, rustic home kitchen, farmhouse props, white lab coat, protective goggles, pipette, beaker, perfume flask, cosmetic laboratory, chef toque, influencer eye contact, staged cooking abundance, decorative handwriting, invented brand slogan, Le Labo-like label, rusty food-contact metal, dirty worktop, loose hair over food, sepia nostalgia, orange-and-teal grade, fog, crushed blacks, spotlight glamour, excessive props, multiple people, multiple actions.

## Acceptance checks

1. The woman reads as the person with judgment authority, not a symbol of decorative femininity.
2. The room reads dark, accumulated and functional while all food-contact zones read safe and clean.
3. The image explains comparison and final verification without laboratory clichés.
4. Input/state, tool, action, intermediate output and record can each be identified.
5. The left copy-safe zone remains usable for a landing-page headline.

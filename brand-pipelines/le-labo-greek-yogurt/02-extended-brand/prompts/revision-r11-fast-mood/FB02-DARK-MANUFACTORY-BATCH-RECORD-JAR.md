# FB02 — Dark Manufactory Batch Record Jar

## Role

Revision 11 fast-mood review candidate for feedback 2: rebuilt brand mood and food-native package. Photorealistic 3:2 landscape commercial/documentary food photography.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape brand-mood photograph inside a working premium Greek-yogurt manufactory. The room has deep graphite shadow, an aged exposed-brick wall and sealed grey concrete on non-food-contact surfaces, with one pristine brushed-stainless food-production island. A neutral grey 4500–5000 K task light falls locally across the product and food materials while the room remains dark but retains shadow detail.

Make one MORA yogurt jar the unmistakable foreground subject, slightly right of center. It is a transparent, low-wide, straight-sided 150 g spoon jar, body width about 1.5 times its height, mouth about 85% of body diameter, fully open, no neck and no shoulders. It must read as a refrigerated spoonable Greek-yogurt vessel before it reads as premium packaging, never as perfume. Preserve plausible thick glass, refraction, rim and base weight.

The jar holds dense cultured-milk-white strained Greek yogurt with a natural broken spoon furrow and subtle dairy grain. Keep at least 60–70% of the edible yogurt visible through unobstructed glass. Attach one restrained partial, slightly fibrous uncoated off-white paper label covering less than 35% of the jar circumference. Only this minimal label text may be legible, rendered verbatim: “MORA”, “BATCH RECORD”, “150 g”. Use an original simple food-production sans-serif hierarchy with open spacing: no boxes, no grid, no dates, no codes, no initials, no certification marks and no other microtext.

Place a small, restrained group of truthful intermediate food materials near but behind the hero jar: one shallow stainless vessel holding pale cultured gel, one small food-grade glass cup of translucent acid whey, and one squat food-safe preparation cup holding a muted amber thyme-honey infusion. Keep them subordinate, partly soft-focused, physically grounded and clearly food-production materials rather than theatrical laboratory glassware. No extra finished jar.

Composition and photographic behavior: medium-wide 70–85 mm equivalent product perspective, natural 25–30 degree downward view, the complete jar and label plane sharp, restrained background depth falloff, clean grounded contact shadows, physically plausible glass refraction and stainless reflections, no bloom, no HDR sheen and no synthetic sharpening. Preserve true cultured-milk white, translucent whey, amber infusion, uncoated paper and steel under the grey task light. Premium comes from work evidence, material restraint and hygienic contrast, not glamour.

## Locked constraints

- Exactly one low-wide transparent open 150 g wide-mouth yogurt jar.
- At least 60–70% of the yogurt remains visible.
- Partial uncoated Batch Record label with only “MORA”, “BATCH RECORD”, “150 g”.
- Dark graphite exposed-brick and sealed-concrete food manufactory.
- Clean brushed-stainless food-contact island.
- Cultured gel, acid whey and thyme-honey infusion remain nearby but subordinate.
- No people, hands, extra finished product, generated dates, codes or microtext.

## Avoid

Perfume bottle, narrow neck, shoulder bottle, fragrance cap, apothecary vessel, dropper, pipette, beaker lineup, pharmacy grid, city/date ritual, ingredient-plus-number naming, Le Labo-like wordmark or trade dress, full-wrap label, fake handwriting, pink or floral femininity, home kitchen, breakfast spread, dirty food-contact surface, unsafe open vat, rust near food, dramatic smoke, decorative condensation, CGI yogurt swirl, honey spectacle, plastic glass, floating objects, crushed blacks, clipped yogurt highlights, extra logos, watermark.

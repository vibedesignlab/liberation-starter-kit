Invisible retouching, natural variation, no HDR sheen, no synthetic sharpening.

## Locked constraints

- Exactly one continuous stainless process table and one left-to-right causal sequence.
- Cultured milk, cloth straining, dense strained base, captured translucent acid whey, food-safe thyme/honey intermediate, broad folding paddle and paper process record are all visible.
- Exactly one pair of adult woman's hands and one active folding action; no face or additional person.
- Scientific authority comes from measurement and state comparison; artisanal authority comes from hands, tools and real food contact.
- Aged brick and sealed concrete are non-food-contact only; every food-contact surface is spotless stainless, food-safe ceramic or clean cloth.
- No legible production claims or dense generated text.

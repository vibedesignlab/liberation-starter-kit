# FB05 — Material Under Care Ambient

## Role

Revision 11 fast-mood review candidate for feedback 5: a photography-led ambient material key visual that compresses MORA's purity, separation, infusion and restrained folding into one real physical food field. Photorealistic 3:2 landscape macro/close commercial food photography; no product package, person or explanatory graphic.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape ambient material key visual titled “Material Under Care”. Build one close physical composition on a dark graphite food-safe stone or blackened-stainless working plane, with a softly resolved sealed-concrete setting beyond it. The image is a real tabletop food photograph, not CGI, not an illustration and not an abstract digital swirl.

Across the center and right two-thirds, form one restrained transformation field from four real edible states that remain individually legible while physically touching:

- a substantial bed of pure cultured-milk-white strained Greek yogurt with dense, naturally broken ridges and tiny dairy grain;
- one shallow translucent channel of pale acid whey separating cleanly from the yogurt edge, with believable surface tension and no splash;
- a thin muted green-gold thyme infusion veil entering that whey channel, with only a few real thyme microfibers suspended or caught at the boundary;
- one narrow amber honey ribbon carried through a single heavy fold in the yogurt, partially embedded rather than poured decoratively over the surface.

The sequence should read from separation to infusion to one final fold without arrows, labels, vessels or hands. The yogurt remains dominant and edible. Acid whey is watery and translucent; thyme infusion is thinner and greener than honey; honey is viscous amber; the fold is heavy and irregular. Preserve small asymmetries, slight moisture, natural thickness, real meniscus and weight deformation so each material behaves physically.

Composition and photographic behavior: close material landscape rather than an extreme microscope view, 70–100 mm macro-like perspective, camera about 25–35 degrees above the plane, moderate depth of field holding the whey boundary, thyme veil and honey fold together while the far graphite/concrete edge falls away softly. Keep the leftmost 20–25% as a quieter graphite/concrete tonal field with no bright honey, thyme fibers or high-contrast ridge, suitable for optional short copy without looking like an empty studio gradient.

Lighting and color: one broad low side task light grazes the food field and reveals clothless dairy ridges, liquid surface tension and the amber fold; restrained soft fill preserves deep graphite shadow detail. Cultured-milk white remains neutral and detailed, acid whey pale and translucent, thyme naturally muted, honey amber rather than orange. Unified shadow direction, plausible specular highlights and continuous falloff; invisible retouching and documentary micro-contrast.

## Locked constraints

- Exactly one continuous physical material field in a 3:2 landscape frame.
- Strained yogurt, translucent acid whey, thyme infusion and amber honey fold are all visible and materially distinct.
- One fold only; no splash, airborne ingredient or repeated spiral.
- Graphite/blackened-steel working plane and softly resolved sealed-concrete setting.
- No jar, packaging, label, paper, person, hand, tool, text, logo or watermark.
- Real food texture and contact physics, not a rendered effect.

## Avoid

Retail jar, bowl rim, bottle, label, batch record, spoon, paddle, hand, person, kitchen scene, ingredient still-life scatter, whole thyme bouquet, honey dipper, decorative garnish, perfume smoke, colored fog, floating particles, splash crown, frozen droplet, levitating food, CGI swirl, perfect spiral, glossy cosmetic cream, whipped shaving-foam peaks, gel sculpture, resin, plastic sheen, marbleized digital texture, gradient mesh, illustrated texture, synthetic caustics, clipped yogurt white, crushed graphite black, sepia, orange-and-teal grade, extra text, logo, watermark.

## Quick sanity checks

- The four edible states are distinguishable without a vessel or explanatory label.
- The single honey fold feels heavy, edible and physically connected to the yogurt.
- The whey/thyme boundary shows real translucency and surface tension.
- The frame reads as low-key material photography, not CGI abstraction or cosmetic cream.

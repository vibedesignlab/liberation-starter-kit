# REF-01 — Manufactory Front Master

## Role

Reusable empty-space master that locks the MORA manufactory identity, strict frontal camera geometry, food-safe material hierarchy, grey-neutral task light and uninterrupted left-side copy field.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape environmental commercial photograph of the EMPTY MORA Greek-yogurt manufactory. This is an architectural space reference, not a product scene or styled kitchen.

Camera geometry is non-negotiable: show a strict 100% FRONT VIEW. Place the camera at 1.4 m height and about 5 m perceived distance with a restrained 34 mm full-frame documentary-interior feel. The sensor plane is exactly parallel to the rear wall and to the long front face of the stainless island. Keep verticals vertical; keep the island front edge and rear-wall shelf lines perfectly horizontal. No oblique view, no corner emphasis, no three-quarter drift, no ultra-wide distortion and no fisheye. Use deep environmental focus with realistic optical detail.

Composition: the LEFT 40% of the entire frame is an uninterrupted low-detail graphite copy-safe field made only from matte sealed-concrete wall flowing into a matte graphite sealed-concrete floor. Most critically, the rectangle spanning approximately x 5–40% and y 30–70% must contain no lamp, shelf, workstation, pipe, fixture, object, cast shadow, reflection, light pool, bright patch or high-contrast joint. Keep the wall-to-floor transition quiet, broad and nearly tone-on-tone, without a sharp seam. Nothing from the right side may overlap or cast into this field.

Confine the working architecture to the RIGHT 55–60%: one clean brushed-stainless food-production island, front-facing and rectilinear, plus a disciplined set of clean controlled stainless shelves against the rear/right area. The island and shelves are infrastructure only and remain empty. Food-contact surfaces are pristine brushed stainless steel. The non-contact shell is aged exposed brick and sealed concrete, with credible wear and construction history but hygienically maintained. Do not decorate the space and do not make it look polished, luxurious, domestic or theatrically distressed.

Light the room with one coherent grey-neutral 4700 K high-CRI task-light family motivated from the upper-right working zone. Preserve nuanced shadow detail in the brick, concrete and brushed metal; no crushed blacks or blown highlights. The right workstation may receive controlled functional illumination, while the left copy field remains evenly dark, matte, low-contrast and reflection-free. Keep color neutral, restrained and materially accurate.

Make scale, straight-line geometry, contact shadows, metal reflections, brick irregularity and sealed-concrete texture physically plausible. Invisible retouching only. The final image should feel like a sober editorial location photograph of a real premium food manufactory between production cycles, with air and working history but no active process.

## Locked constraints

- New photorealistic 3:2 landscape environmental master.
- Empty MORA manufactory with strict 100% front view.
- Sensor plane parallel to rear wall and island face; no three-quarter drift.
- 34 mm feel, camera height about 1.4 m, perceived distance about 5 m.
- Left 40% uninterrupted low-detail graphite wall/floor copy field.
- Right-side clean stainless island and controlled shelves only.
- Aged brick and sealed concrete only on non-food-contact shell.
- One grey-neutral 4700 K task-light family with detailed shadows.

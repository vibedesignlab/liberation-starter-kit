# REF-04 — Object Scale Kit

## Role

Reusable neutral object-scale reference kit derived from REF-05. It calibrates the preserved MORA jar against ordinary food-production tools on one ground plane. Directional dimensions guide proportion only.

## Edit prompt

Image 1 is the approved REF-05 MORA product master and the exact identity source for the jar.

Recompose Image 1 into a photorealistic 3:2 landscape neutral reference-kit photograph. Preserve the exact jar identity: same low-wide transparent 150 g vessel, body proportions, wide open mouth, glass wall and base, cultured-milk-white yogurt, spoon furrow, partial uncoated label, exact “MORA” and “150 g” text, and no other label copy. Do not redesign, stretch, enlarge or shrink the jar relative to its directional 80–90 mm diameter and 60–70 mm height.

Place the preserved jar on a seamless neutral light-grey food-studio ground with four ordinary scale references on the same physical surface and shared baseline:

1. One brushed-stainless dessert spoon, directional length 145–160 mm, approximately 1.8 times the jar diameter, lying fully visible in front of the jar.
2. One plain brushed-stainless mixing bowl, directional diameter 200–240 mm, approximately 2.5 times the jar diameter, sitting behind-left without hiding the jar.
3. One broad stainless folding paddle, directional length 250–320 mm, approximately 3.4 times the jar diameter, lying horizontally behind the kit and fully inside frame; it is a short food-production paddle, not a shovel, oar or giant kitchen utensil.
4. One A6-sized uncoated off-white Batch Record sheet, directional size 105 × 148 mm, lying flat or at a slight supported angle on the right. It may show only a few faint open rule lines and one blank heading bar; no readable words, dates, numbers, codes, boxes, grid or microtext.

Use a 70–90 mm equivalent medium product-reference perspective and a restrained 25–30 degree downward view. Keep every object complete, clearly separated and grounded with plausible contact shadows. Maintain relative scale: jar smaller than bowl, spoon and paddle; no oversized jar and no giant tools. Use neutral 4500–5000 K high-CRI light, controlled stainless reflections, physically plausible glass refraction and true cultured-milk white. The frame is a calibration reference, not a styled recipe scene.

## Change only

Change the neutral single-product composition into the five-object scale kit. Preserve jar identity, label, yogurt, glass, color response and product construction from Image 1.

## Avoid

Person, hand, ruler, dimension text, numbers, fake Batch Record data, readable microtext, ingredient props, additional jar, closed jar, cap, perfume silhouette, beaker, pipette, theatrical lab glassware, kitchen scene, dark campaign mood, bowl smaller than jar, spoon larger than bowl, giant paddle, floating tools, mismatched ground planes, inconsistent shadows, distorted metal, clipped objects, watermark.

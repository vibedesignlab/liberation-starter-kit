# REF-05 — Product Master

## Role

Reusable neutral PDP/reference master that locks the MORA 150 g jar identity, proportions, transparent food witness, open spoon access and restrained paper-label behavior. This is a directional scale reference, not an approved production specification.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape neutral PDP/reference photograph of exactly one MORA 150 g Greek-yogurt jar. Center the complete product silhouette on a seamless neutral light-grey studio ground with even outer margins and no contextual props.

The vessel is a low-wide, straight-sided transparent food jar with a directional real-world body diameter of 80–90 mm and body height of 60–70 mm. Its mouth is nearly the full body diameter, visibly open and immediately usable with a dessert spoon. There is no neck, shoulder, cap, lid, pump or fragrance-bottle anatomy. Keep the top opening and clean wide rim visible from a restrained 15–20 degree downward product view while preserving the authoritative front face.

Fill the jar with dense cultured-milk-white strained Greek yogurt. The food remains visible through most of the clear glass and has a natural slightly irregular dairy grain, one restrained broken spoon furrow and a stable ridge. No ingredient, honey, herb, fruit, garnish or decorative swirl appears. Preserve realistic glass wall thickness, rim, base weight, fill line and physically plausible refraction.

Attach one partial, slightly fibrous uncoated off-white paper label to the lower front-right area, covering less than one third of the vessel circumference and leaving the majority of yogurt unobstructed. Only this text may be readable, rendered verbatim: “MORA” and “150 g”. Use simple original food-production sans-serif typography. No other words, initials, dates, numbers, marks, boxes, grid, symbols or microtext.

Camera and light: 70–90 mm equivalent short-telephoto product-photography feel, natural perspective, medium framing, complete centered silhouette, label and rim sharp, restrained depth falloff behind the product. Use neutral 4500–5000 K high-CRI studio light with controlled vertical highlight gradients that describe transparent glass without hiding the food. Keep grounded contact shadow, visible highlight detail and true cultured-milk white. Invisible retouching only; preserve natural food and paper texture.

## Locked constraints

- Exactly one complete centered 150 g jar.
- Directional body diameter 80–90 mm and body height 60–70 mm.
- Mouth nearly body diameter, fully open and spoonable.
- Dense plain cultured-milk-white Greek yogurt visible through most glass.
- Partial uncoated paper label with only “MORA” and “150 g”.
- Neutral PDP/reference ground and color-accurate light.

## Avoid

Perfume silhouette, narrow neck, shoulder bottle, fragrance cap, apothecary styling, jar larger than the stated directional scale, ingredient styling, topping, honey, herb, fruit, spoon prop, person, hand, kitchen, manufactory set, dark campaign mood, full-wrap label, pharmacy grid, fake production data, microtext, logo other than MORA, plastic-looking glass, impossible reflections, floating product, CGI yogurt swirl, crushed whites, watermark.

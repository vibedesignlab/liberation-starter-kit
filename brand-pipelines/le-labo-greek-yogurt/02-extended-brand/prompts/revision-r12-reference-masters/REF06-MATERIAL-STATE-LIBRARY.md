# REF-06 — Material State Library

## Role

Revision 12 reusable factual material-state master. This is a color-accurate comparison library for later product, process and Storybook review work—not a final macro key visual, campaign still life or documentary process claim.

## Generation prompt

Create a new photorealistic 3:2 landscape commercial food-reference photograph showing five Greek-yogurt material states as a strict front-facing comparison library.

Scene and ground: use one clean brushed-stainless food-contact work surface with a quiet sealed-concrete vertical background in medium graphite-grey. The workbench front edge and rear wall are perfectly horizontal. The camera sensor plane is square and parallel to the rear wall and the shared vessel baseline: no horizontal three-quarter drift, no diagonal table edge, no overhead flat lay. Use a restrained 70–90 mm medium-detail product-photography perspective with modest compression and no wide-angle distortion.

Subjects: place exactly five modest-scale, unlabeled, food-safe preparation containers on one straight shared baseline. Keep equal center-to-center spacing, similar visual weight, consistent rim height and complete unobstructed silhouettes. Each container is low and wide, with an open mouth or transparent food-storage wall appropriate to its contents; none has a narrow neck, pouring spout, laboratory graduations or perfume-bottle shoulders.

Arrange left to right:

1. Cultured milk / early cultured gel in a low, wide, straight-sided transparent food-storage crock; opaque cultured-milk-white liquid with a soft just-set surface and slight natural meniscus.
2. Dense strained Greek yogurt in a shallow brushed-stainless prep bowl; heavy cultured-milk-white ridges rise just above the rim and visibly hold shape.
3. Captured translucent acid whey in a squat, wide-mouthed clear food-storage cup; pale straw-white translucency, real liquid meniscus and restrained refraction.
4. Filtered muted thyme infusion in a second squat, wide-mouthed food-safe clear prep cup of comparable scale; transparent muted green-gold liquid, fully filtered with only an extremely faint natural tint and no decorative herb sprigs.
5. Restrained thyme-honey fold preparation in a low off-white food-safe ceramic prep bowl; dense strained base carrying one narrow embedded amber honey fold and only a few subtle thyme microfibers, not a finished plated dessert.

The five states must differ through opacity, viscosity, surface relief, translucency and actual ingredient color rather than container decoration. Preserve believable fill levels, liquid surface tension, material thickness, weight and contact. Containers sit naturally on the steel with small physically coherent contact shadows and no floating or fused edges.

Lighting and capture: neutral 4500–5000 K color-accurate studio/task light, one broad soft key slightly above and in front, controlled fill and restrained black flags for shape. Keep cultured-milk white neutral and detailed, whey and thyme infusion translucent but distinct, amber fold restrained, stainless neutral. Medium depth of field keeps all five containers and contents comparably sharp. Controlled reflections reveal clear-wall thickness and brushed steel without glare hiding the food. Basic exposure and white-balance correction only; no creative color grade, dramatic vignette, haze, bloom, HDR sheen or crushed blacks.

## Locked constraints

- Exactly five food-safe containers on one straight baseline with equal spacing.
- Strict front comparison: rear wall, bench face, horizon and shared baseline remain parallel and horizontal.
- Cultured milk/gel, dense strained yogurt, acid whey, filtered thyme infusion and thyme-honey fold preparation are each visibly distinct.
- Neutral 4500–5000 K light and reusable color-accurate factual presentation.
- No person, hand, tool, packaging, product jar, label, text, logo or watermark.
- This remains a medium-detail library; no extreme macro crop or final ambient art direction.

## Avoid

Beaker, Erlenmeyer flask, graduated cylinder, test tube, pipette, dropper, chemistry rack, measuring marks, perfume labware, apothecary bottle, narrow neck, bottle stopper, identical clear-liquid lineup, decorative whole ingredients, thyme bouquet, honey dipper, spoon, cloth, scale, process record, label, typography, finished retail product, plated dessert, multiple rows, staggered depth, three-quarter drift, diagonal baseline, overhead flat lay, CGI food, cosmetic cream swirl, plastic liquid, floating container, dirty steel, rust near food, warm sepia, orange-and-teal grade, bright hotspot, deep crushed shadow, clipped white, vignette, watermark.

## Quick sanity checks

- Five and only five containers appear on one comparable baseline.
- Each state reads by physical behavior rather than text or decoration.
- The camera reads strict front and the food stays color accurate.

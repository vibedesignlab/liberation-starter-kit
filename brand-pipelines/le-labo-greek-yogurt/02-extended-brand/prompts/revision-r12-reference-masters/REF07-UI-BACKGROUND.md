# REF-07 — UI Product Background

## Role

Revision 12 reusable empty UI/product staging ground. This master supplies a quiet, color-accurate physical wall-and-surface environment for later product compositing or generation on either side; it is not a finished hero, campaign set or mood image.

## Generation prompt

Create a new photorealistic empty 3:2 landscape studio/product background master with no subject.

Physical scene: one restrained vertical background plane transitions subtly from deep neutral graphite in the upper area to medium sealed-concrete grey near the horizon. It meets one clean horizontal brushed-stainless work surface across the lower 35–40% of the frame. The wall-to-surface meeting line is perfectly straight, physically coherent and horizontal. The two planes have believable scale, thickness, finish and perspective: a sealed concrete wall is vertical; brushed stainless is horizontal. No cyclorama curve, floating plane, pedestal or raised plinth.

Composition: strict straight-on studio view using a restrained 70–90 mm product-photography lens feel. Camera sensor plane remains parallel to the vertical wall and the horizon; no three-quarter drift, diagonal surface edge, wide-angle convergence or overhead view. Keep the center calm and both the left third and right third equally open, low-detail and usable for future product placement. Neither side receives a brighter pool, darker vignette, reflection streak, seam, texture knot or architectural edge that would force composition. Maintain generous continuous clear placement zones across both outer thirds.

Lighting and color: a broad neutral grey 4500–5000 K studio/task-light family creates a very subtle top-to-bottom and rear-to-front tonal gradient, enough to separate wall from surface without a visible spotlight. Use large soft sources and balanced fill so illumination is nearly symmetrical left-to-right. Graphite retains detail instead of falling to black; sealed concrete remains neutral grey; stainless stays clean and neutral with only a soft broad reflection gradient. Preserve highlight and shadow detail, correct white balance, one physically plausible light family and smooth falloff.

Surface character: the wall has extremely restrained sealed-concrete microtexture at low contrast; the stainless plane has fine horizontal brushing visible only on close inspection. Keep both surfaces factual and easy to composite over. No dust, scratches that become focal points, stains, water, condensation, food residue or decorative distress.

## Locked constraints

- Empty 3:2 landscape physical background with no subject or prop.
- Vertical graphite-to-sealed-concrete ground plus horizontal clean brushed-stainless surface.
- Straight coherent horizon/contact line and strict front 70–90 mm product-studio perspective.
- Both left and right thirds remain equally usable for future product placement.
- Neutral 4500–5000 K, low-detail, color-accurate, no cinematic crushed black or bright hotspot.
- No product, person, text, logo or watermark.

## Avoid

Product, jar, bowl, spoon, ingredient, cloth, paper, record, label, person, hand, furniture, shelf, brick, doorway, window, visible lamp, signage, text, logo, watermark, pedestal, podium, plinth, block, step, corner room, cyclorama sweep, horizon curve, diagonal table edge, split panels, frame, border, strong seam, bright center spotlight, one-sided hotspot, dramatic cast shadow, mirror reflection, colored gel light, warm beige wash, sepia, orange-and-teal grade, blue cast, fog, smoke, dust, food residue, water, rust, decorative scratches, high-frequency concrete pattern, crushed black, clipped steel highlight, vignette, CGI gradient mesh.

## Quick sanity checks

- The image is genuinely empty and reads as two physically coherent planes.
- The horizon is straight and the camera reads strict front.
- Both outer thirds remain calm, low-detail and equally usable.

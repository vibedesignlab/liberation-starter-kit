# REF-08 — Batch Record Label Master

## Role

PNG visual reference for every partial paper record appearing on a MORA jar, Finish Table, product proof or UI composition. It communicates the label's paper color, open information hierarchy, partial application and operational role; it is not production artwork.

## Visual construction

- Strict 100% front, 3:2 reference canvas with a single isolated 72 × 48 mm directional label.
- Greyed uncoated-paper candidate `#C9BDA5`, neutral graphite factual ink and restrained paper grain.
- Workhorse grotesk with tabular-number behavior; no condensed, stencil, typewriter or pharmacy-label imitation.
- Open alignment, spacing and two main rules instead of boxing every field into a laboratory grid.
- Fixed print fields: `RECIPE`, `BATCH`, `MILK LOT`, `CULTURED`, `STRAINED`, `FOLDED`, `PACKED`, `KEEP COLD`, `NET WT`.
- Limited live marking fields: `MAKER INITIALS`, `FINAL CHECK`, `BATCH CONDITION`.
- The reference leaves variable values blank and identifies itself as a concept requiring R&D, operations and legal validation.

## Application rule

Attach this paper to only part of the low-wide transparent jar. It must never become a full wrap, sleeve or large opaque front panel. The product must retain at least 60–70% visible food area; paper coverage stays at or below the directional 30–40% maximum. Legal, allergen, nutrition, storage, responsibility and date information remains standardized and separate from handwriting.

## Reference-use instruction

Use this PNG as the label-identity and information-hierarchy reference only. When paired with a product, room or mood reference, preserve its paper color, open field rhythm, partial coverage and restricted maker-check zone. Do not create per-SKU SVG files and do not copy the graphite canvas as product-scene styling.

## Avoid

Decorative brand sticker, blank white card, customer name as ornament, city/date ritual, fake signature, preprinted handwriting, fictitious batch values, full-wrap paper, opaque sleeve, exact Le Labo grid or typography, apothecary cues, kraft-farmhouse styling, colored flavor bands and food-obscuring application.

## Provenance

Project-local PNG visual reference prepared without browser automation on 2026-08-31. Directional concept only; not production-ready label artwork or a packaging specification.

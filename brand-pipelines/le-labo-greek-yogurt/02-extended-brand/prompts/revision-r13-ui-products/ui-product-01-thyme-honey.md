# UI Product 01 — Thyme Honey

## Production intent

Create one photorealistic 3:2 landscape UI product master for a collection grid and PDP comparison system. This is a neutral product-truth frame, not a campaign still life. Render exactly one open 150 g Greek-yogurt jar, centered and shown strict front, against the locked MORA UI background. Preserve enough negative space that the frame can later sit beside five sibling SKUs without recropping.

## Supplied references and strict role separation

1. **REF-07 — UI background only.** Reproduce its dark-to-mid graphite concrete wall, horizontal wall-to-stainless ground line, brushed stainless counter, neutral exposure, soft falloff, and restrained surface reflection. Do not transfer any product or prop from elsewhere into this background.
2. **REF-05 — jar geometry only.** Use its low-wide transparent 150 g glass jar, approximately 80–90 mm diameter by 60–70 mm body height, with a mouth nearly as wide as the body and a clearly spoonable open top. Preserve its believable glass thickness and food volume. Do not copy its light-grey studio background, camera angle, generic `MORA / 150 g` sticker, sticker size, sticker placement, or typography.
3. **REF-08 — partial Batch Record role only.** Use only its warm off-white uncoated paper color, restrained black information hierarchy, open line rhythm, partial jar coverage, and tiny restricted maker-check zone. Translate these qualities into a newly composed food-native record label. Do not reproduce its graphite canvas, flat-sheet composition, exact grid, exact wording, filled values, dates, codes, legal data, or trade dress.
4. **REF-06 — food material only.** Use its cultured-milk ivory color and dense strained-yogurt viscosity as the base, plus the believable translucency and viscosity of its amber liquid as the honey reference. Do not transfer its containers, lineup, concrete background, table position, or camera view.

## Composition and camera lock

- Final raster: 1536 × 1024 px, 3:2 landscape.
- One jar only, centered on the same ground plane and at the same horizontal ground line as REF-07.
- Strict front elevation: the camera sensor is square to the label plane and background; jar left and right edges are symmetrical. Only the minimum top visibility needed to prove the mouth is open and wide.
- Natural 70–90 mm full-frame lens feel with no wide-angle exaggeration.
- Complete jar silhouette and contact shadow must remain visible.
- Jar height is 28–32% of the full frame height, approximately 287–328 px in a 1024 px-high frame. It must read as a small 150 g spoon jar, never as a candle, perfume bottle, tumbler, or storage crock.

## Product and food truth

- Clear, low-wide glass jar; open mouth; no lid in frame; no neck, shoulders, cap, atomizer, stopper, or perfume silhouette.
- Food remains visible through most of the glass around the partial label.
- Fill with dense cultured-milk-white Greek yogurt: matte ivory, faint natural curd grain, softly folded surface, spoonable rather than glossy or whipped.
- Add one thin amber honey fold within the yogurt, visible as a narrow irregular ribbon rather than a pool, drip, glaze, or syrup flood.
- Add sparse actual-scale thyme flecks within the fold: tiny muted green-brown fragments, not whole sprigs, leaves, garnish, confetti, or botanical styling.
- No ingredient props, spoon, hands, cloth, cutting board, flowers, fruit, bowls, process vessels, or decorative food scatter.

## Partial Batch Record label

- Attach one restrained partial label to the front lower-right or front-right quadrant, covering no more than about 30–40% of the jar face so the yogurt remains the primary visible material.
- Use warm off-white uncoated paper with subtle real fibers, a dry edge, slight contact waviness, and physically believable adhesion against curved cold glass.
- It must read at UI size as a structured food record through hierarchy and open rule lines, not as a perfume label, pharmacy grid, oversized data form, or generic brand sticker.
- The only deliberately readable text may be modest `MORA`, `THYME HONEY`, and a small `BATCH RECORD`. Any subordinate fields must be tiny, sparse, and not promoted into large generated microtext; prefer short open leader lines and blank field rhythm. Allow at most one tiny check mark in a restricted maker-check area.
- No `150 g` copied from REF-05, no fake dates, lot numbers, barcodes, recycling marks, temperature claims, initials, signatures, city/date ritual, serial codes, legal claims, circular seals, ruled regulatory column, or dense pharmacy-style boxes.

## Lighting and photographic behavior

- Neutral high-CRI 4500–5000 K light consistent with REF-07; preserve food color accuracy inside the dark graphite environment.
- Soft frontal-side key and restrained overhead lift: controlled highlight bands on glass, legible label paper, ivory yogurt without blue or yellow cast, and an amber fold that remains translucent.
- Grounded contact shadow and restrained stainless reflection directly below the jar; no floating, halo, bloom, dramatic spotlight, crushed black, teal-orange grade, cinematic haze, or excessive depth blur.
- Natural commercial photography with realistic glass refraction and microscopic imperfections. Avoid plastic-looking glass, airbrushed yogurt, hyper-sharp CGI edges, luxury fragrance styling, apothecary cues, and artificial perfection.

## Acceptance target

The output must read immediately as one open, low-wide 150 g Greek-yogurt jar on the locked REF-07 UI stage. The strict-front silhouette, 28–32% frame-height scale, partial uncoated food-record label, visible ivory yogurt, thin amber honey fold, and sparse actual-scale thyme flecks must all be present in the same image.

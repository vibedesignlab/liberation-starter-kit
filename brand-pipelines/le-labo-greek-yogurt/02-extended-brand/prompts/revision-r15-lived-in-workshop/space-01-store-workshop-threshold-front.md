# SPACE-01 — Store to Workshop Threshold

Use case: photorealistic-natural  
Asset type: MORA reusable spatial reference master

All four S1 inputs are style-and-operational-logic references only. Do not copy their brand, signage, bottle walls, shelving grid, architecture, labels, or recognizable trade dress.

Photograph a believable compact urban premium Greek-yogurt atelier where a small storefront and the working fermentation room share one real address. Strict 100% front, 3:2 landscape, eye level 1.45 m, 40–50 mm, perceived distance 4–6 m. Show a narrow public counter and threshold, a transparent but non-identical steel-and-wired-glass separation, and the back food workshop. Combine repaired chalky plaster, irregular utility tile, sealed worn concrete, darkened timber, an existing doorway and street-facing window. Food-contact surfaces alone are clean brushed stainless.

Group food-safe tubs, folded cloths, ingredient crates, batch papers, trolley, cooler, hooks and cleaning tools by real task. Preserve a left-center copy field using low-contrast repaired plaster and doorway shadow, never a seamless blank wall. Use cool available daylight plus localized neutral-warm practical lights, uneven falloff and restrained documentary grain.

Avoid people, hero product, logos, legible brand text, perfume/apothecary props, cyclorama, television studio, showroom, giant blank graphite wall, identical stainless modules, perfect symmetry, full-room studio fill, farmhouse nostalgia and exact Le Labo trade dress.

# SPACE-03 — Batch Archive Workwall

Use case: photorealistic-natural  
Asset type: MORA reusable spatial reference master

Use S1 only for working inventory, material aging and task-based accumulation. Invent a food-native MORA system; do not copy bottle walls, drawer proportions, labels, signage, grids or recognizable source-brand trade dress.

Photograph a strict-front working archive wall in a compact premium Greek-yogurt atelier. 3:2 landscape, eye level 1.45 m, 50–60 mm. Repaired utility tile transitions into patched plaster; built-in and freestanding shelves do not align perfectly. Store folded food-grade cloths, ingredient lots in kraft sleeves and dark food-safe jars, stackable tubs, batch clipboards too small to read, spare cartons, analog scale, hooks, task lamp and a pass-through opening to the finishing bench. Dense clusters are separated by real task zones; surfaces show handling while contact equipment stays sanitary.

Use low cool daylight and one warm-neutral articulated task light. Avoid legible brand names, decorative handwriting, fragrance bottles, pharmacy labels, copied Le Labo display, perfect grid, boutique merchandising, spotless shelving, television set, blank wall and rustic kitchen styling.

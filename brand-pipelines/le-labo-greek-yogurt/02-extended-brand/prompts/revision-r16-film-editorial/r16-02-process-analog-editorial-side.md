## Edit instruction

Use case: precise-object-edit.

**Change only the analog capture, scan and print response of Image 1. Keep everything else the same.** Do not recompose, restage, relight or redraw the process scene.

### Analog capture and print response

- Make the photograph read as photorealistic 35 mm high-speed color-negative documentary capture, visually equivalent to an ISO 800 small-frame negative scanned for an independent food/culture magazine.
- Add clearly visible but restrained irregular organic film grain. Grain is luminance-led and physically uneven: coarser and more apparent in repaired plaster, utility tile, black sleeves, stainless midtones and open shadows; finer and less intrusive in bright yogurt, pale whey, paper and window highlights. It must not look like a uniform monochrome digital-noise layer.
- Lift the black floor slightly so foreground steel, black workwear and deep shelving remain open and textured rather than crushed. Preserve natural midtone separation.
- Give bright yogurt, paper, practical lamp and window a soft color-negative highlight shoulder with retained material detail, never clipped white.
- Allow modest frame-local color-density variation and minute scan texture while keeping cultured white, pale whey, green-gold infusion, grey paper and stainless steel factually legible.
- Apply localized gentle halation/bloom only immediately around the existing bright street-window edge and existing practical lamp. Keep it subtle and neutral-warm; no global haze and no red neon halo.
- Add mild lens/scan softness, natural micro-contrast and tiny focus falloff toward the extreme frame edges. The hands, paddle contact, cloth-held concentrate, captured whey, dense yogurt, infusion and record remain readable and correctly focused.
- Preserve the existing cool available daylight versus restrained warm task-light relationship. No teal-orange split, sepia nostalgia, bleached fashion grade or generalized warm cast.
- Retain documentary imperfection already present: stainless micro-scratches, small plaster repairs, irregular tile, cloth creases, minor food asymmetry, imperfect overlaps and foreground occlusion. Do not clean, beautify or symmetrize them.

### Absolute preserve lock

- Preserve exact 1536 × 1024 composition and crop, strict 90-degree side-facing camera axis, perspective, horizon, room geometry, wall/window/door positions and left copy-safe architecture.
- Preserve exact hands, fingers, sleeves, pose, occlusion and paddle contact. Do not add the maker's face, body or any new person.
- Preserve exact work sequence and count: cultured base; cloth-held concentrate; captured whey; dense strained base; one recipe infusion; one paddle; one record. Do not add, remove, move, resize, replace or relabel any element.
- Preserve every bowl, tray, cloth, jar/container, paper, bucket, towel, shelf, lamp and table at the same physical scale and position.
- Preserve original light direction, object shadows, reflections, glass/liquid behavior, food texture and paper meaning. The capture change must not alter physical lighting or create a new source.
- Preserve all existing text/marks as-is; generate no new typography, dates, process values, labels, logos, captions or magazine layout.
- Image 2 is never a compositing donor. Do not import its woman, writing gesture, table, bowl, whey glass, window, architecture or front-facing composition.

## 3. Photography series lock — Quiet Intensity

Series mode: web-first commercial photography, photorealistic, contemporary cultured-food atelier.

### Shared visual behavior

- Move from warm craft / beige café toward a cool-neutral, immersive modern food atelier.
- Keep color neutral rather than blue: mineral charcoal, cool gray plaster, brushed stainless steel, clear glass, white food-safe cloth, actual food color.
- Warm color is permitted only when it comes from real ingredients such as honey, grain, citrus, or yogurt—not from the grade, walls, wood, or decorative light.
- One motivated upper-left window source; directional soft-to-firm light; approximately natural 4:1 modeling; selective negative fill; shadow detail retained.
- Preserve stainless micro-scratches, cloth weave, yogurt weight, whey translucency, physically plausible glass reflection and grounded contact.
- Invisible retouching; no uniform gloss, procedural texture, HDR clarity, crushed black, clipped highlight, wax skin, or CGI-perfect symmetry.
- No decorative herb vase, rustic pottery, warm wood, linen lifestyle styling, amber tungsten, golden hour, beige café, apothecary props, perfume cues, laboratory cosplay, text, logos, or watermark.

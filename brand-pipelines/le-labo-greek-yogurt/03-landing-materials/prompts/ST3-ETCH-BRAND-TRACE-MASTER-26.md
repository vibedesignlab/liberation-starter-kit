# ST3-ETCH-BRAND-TRACE-MASTER-26 — The Trace That Remains

## Role and rationale

This is the Wave A golden master for the MORA Material Folios illustration series. Its only communication job is to make the brand proposition readable without copy: **process → trace → density**.

The two Le Journal inputs are source-brand evidence for line discipline, monochrome stipple/cross-hatching and the confidence to leave large empty areas. Their house, bottle, doorway, travel scene, characters and collage structures are not compositional references and must not reappear. MORA translates one source principle only: a real material state makes exactly one surreal but legible transition into another state.

## Input-image roles

- Image 1 — `VE-EC-014-le-journal-house-rules.png`: line-density and copperplate/cross-hatch behavior reference only; never copy the house, bottle, suitcase or object arrangement.
- Image 2 — `VE-EC-015-le-journal-wish-you-wore-here.png`: stipple, contour restraint and negative-space confidence reference only; never copy the bottle, door, landscape or portal composition.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story.

Asset type: landscape 3:2 editorial brand-manifesto illustration and style golden master for a premium Greek-yogurt landing page.

Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 are style evidence only. Translate their disciplined fine engraving line, restrained cross-hatching, stipple and large white-space logic. Do not reproduce, remix or quote any house, bottle, doorway, travel scene, suitcase, landscape, character, label, wording or original composition from either input.

Scene and paper: a completely clean contemporary warm-cream field in exact MORA Cultured Cream `#F5F1E8`, flat and unframed, without paper edge, grain, stain, shadow, foxing, deckle or faux age.

Primary request: draw exactly one continuous impossible material transition across a low horizontal band. At the left, a compact folded straining cloth holds a small gravity-directed mass of cultured curd, shown with believable textile weave, weight and downward tension. From the cloth’s lowest fold, the curd thins into one continuous fine contour accompanied by only three to five restrained descending droplets and sparse stipple—the trace. That same trace travels rightward, gently thickens, and resolves at the right into one smooth single-spoon portion of concentrated Greek yogurt with one clean held furrow and one soft folded ridge—the density. The left cloth/curd, middle trace and right yogurt fold must read as one causal continuum, not three separate icons and not a process diagram.

Style and palette: refined monochrome copperplate etching with modern editorial exactness; only fine MORA ink `#171714` on `#F5F1E8`. Crisp controlled contours, restrained cross-hatching, restrained stipple, coherent single engraving-light direction, visible midtone detail and natural material asymmetry. Keep line weights fine and breathable; no large filled black region.

Composition and crop: landscape 3:2. Keep the complete material continuum inside the central-lower horizontal band, spanning approximately x 16–84% and y 43–70%. Leave 65–70% of the entire canvas as uninterrupted warm-cream negative space, especially above and around the continuum. The cloth is the small dominant real tool group on the left, the trace is narrow and quiet through the center, and the final yogurt furrow is compact on the right. No crop touches any object; no surrounding scene; no decorative balancing object.

Information test: even without words, the eye must read from left to right as process → retained trace → concentrated body. Exactly one impossible transformation is permitted: cloth-held curd becomes trace and the same trace becomes the final yogurt fold.

Constraints: no text anywhere inside the artwork, no letters, numerals, captions, arrows, legends, logo, wordmark, signature, watermark, frame or sentence. No second transformation, second process, multiple panels, icons, flowchart, human, hand, face, founder portrait, MORA vessel, perfume bottle, food jar, house, door, portal, suitcase, landscape, travel cue, botanical specimen or ingredient pile.

Avoid: faux aging, sepia, parchment, yellow-brown vintage cast, Victorian border, crest, ornamental cartouche, apothecary label, pharmacy grid, dense botanical illustration, heavy woodcut black mass, rustic nostalgia, barnboard, linen backdrop, antique bookplate, occult/alchemical symbol, whimsical storybook style, tattoo flash, clip art, vector icon, CGI, photorealism or heavy gray wash.

Output: `assets/editorial-etchings/mora-etching-brand-trace-master.png`

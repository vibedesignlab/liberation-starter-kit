# MORA Hero Craft Space — Series Lock

## Purpose

Four photorealistic 16:9 landing hero candidates that make MORA craftsmanship legible through a real yogurt-making action and the functional order of the space. The source-brand influence is limited to an open workshop, visible tools, one practiced action and honest material wear. Do not reproduce source interiors, fixtures, signage, packaging or trade dress.

## Web role and composition

- Web UI role: landing-page hero.
- Information goal: this is a premium Greek-yogurt atelier where one maker visibly transforms cultured milk by hand-assisted, measured food work.
- Frame: wide 16:9 landscape, eye-level or very slightly elevated, wide-normal perspective without ultra-wide distortion.
- Copy-safe rule: left 38–42% remains one continuous low-detail Cultured Cream wall/air zone. No person, tool, prop, cast shadow, reflection or high-contrast edge enters it.
- Subject zone: maker and one active workstation stay in the right 58–62%.
- Responsive crop: the central 4:3 crop must retain the maker's action and primary tool; the left desktop crop retains headline space.

## Fixed space identity

- Contemporary urban dairy atelier, not a restaurant kitchen, farmhouse, perfume laboratory or industrial factory.
- Honest lime-plaster walls in warm Cultured Cream, a limited area of milk-white glazed tile only where food work requires cleaning, slim blackened-steel shelving and frames, brushed food-grade stainless worktops, dark charcoal lower cabinets, clear food-safe glass and off-white filter cloth.
- One restrained adjustable task lamp may support a workstation. No decorative furniture, nostalgia props, pharmacy drawers, atomizers, amber tincture bottles or staged luxury objects.
- Tools are sparse and functionally grouped: lidded stainless vessel, calibrated bench scale with unreadable display, dosing jug, perforated draining frame, muslin, catch pan, broad paddle, shallow bowl, cooling rack and wide-mouth clear glass food vessels.

## Fixed maker identity

- One adult woman maker consistent with the existing MORA process series: dark hair tied back, black cotton T-shirt and plain charcoal apron, natural face and hands, no jewelry, no gloves unless the action physically requires them.
- She is a generated maker, never captioned as the founder.
- One credible action only; gaze follows the work rather than the camera.

## Capture and light

- Photorealistic commercial documentary image with restrained polish.
- Wide-normal 35 mm full-frame behavior, medium-wide distance, readable spatial depth, moderate depth of field: maker, active tool and immediate workstation sharp; distant shelves gently softer but still coherent.
- One large overcast industrial-window source from upper left, optional dim practical task light at the station, unified shadow direction, retained midtone detail and no crushed blacks.
- Neutral-warm dairy whites, charcoal, stainless and clear glass; actual ingredient color is the only accent.
- Natural grain, invisible retouching, small fabric creases, subtle steel use marks, plausible food weight and contact deformation.

## Physical QA

- Hands, fingers and tool grip are anatomically plausible.
- Every vessel rests on one ground plane with correct contact and occlusion shadows.
- Stainless highlights share one source; glass refraction and reflections are physically plausible.
- Yogurt is dense, matte-satin and edible, not foam, paint, liquid cream or plastic.
- Equipment scale and perspective are consistent; no impossible tubing, duplicate tools, floating vessels or fake machine interfaces.

## Avoid

No LE LABO wording or logos, perfume bottle geometry, atomizer, narrow-neck bottle, paper perfume label, pharmacy grid, apothecary display, whiskey, spirit, tincture, amber glass, cosmetic labware, hospital laboratory, rustic farmhouse kitchen, country ceramics overload, vintage sepia, luxury gold, cluttered prop styling, multiple workers, customer dining scene, fake founder, fake readable label, batch number, city, date, temperature, pH or legal text, unsupported handmade/small-batch/organic/health/sustainability claim, HDR, CGI gloss, waxy skin, uniform procedural texture, watermark or generated typography.

## Variation axis

Change only the active workstation and its one action:

1. Straining Wall — cloth, draining frame and whey separation.
2. Infusion Bench — ingredient-specific measuring and preparation.
3. Folding Island — broad-paddle low-shear fold and visible trace.
4. Filling & Record Counter — portion, seal alignment and final visual check.

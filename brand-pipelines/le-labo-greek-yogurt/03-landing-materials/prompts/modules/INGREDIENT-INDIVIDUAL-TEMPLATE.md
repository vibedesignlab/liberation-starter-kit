# MORA Individual Ingredient Reference Template

## Communication job

Give every recipe one isolated, reusable material-truth image. These six frames replace reliance on a single group atlas and provide the ingredient reference used by every later SKU variation.

## Locked prompt

`[INGREDIENT_SUBJECT]`, accurately recognizable and naturally irregular, presented alone in one identical low circular optically clear glass specimen dish. True 90° aerial commercial material portrait, 100 mm macro equivalent, deep enough focus to read the whole subject. The dish is centered at x 50%, y 50% within ±2 percentage points and the complete subject/dish grouping matches the outer bounding box x 28–72%, y 28–72% within ±4% scale. One large soft upper-left 4500 K source with delicate frontal fill, controlled glass rim and a soft contact shadow down-right. Plain seamless matte Cultured Cream `#F5F1E8` surface filling the entire frame. Neutral-warm food color, honest raw texture, fine natural grain, 1536 × 1536. No group atlas, no unrelated ingredient, no package, no label, no hand, no table scene, no gradient, no horizon, no decorative prop, no rustic or laboratory styling.`

## Series test

Contact-sheet the six frames in a 3 × 2 grid. Reject a frame if its dish diameter, center, aerial axis, background, light direction or subject occupancy reads differently from the other five. Recipe-specific texture may vary; staging may not.

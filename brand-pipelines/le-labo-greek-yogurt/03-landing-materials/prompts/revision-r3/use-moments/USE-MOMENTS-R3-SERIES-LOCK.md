# MORA Use Moments R3 Series Lock

## Scope

Bounded Stage 3 use-moment series for IDs `ST3-MOMENT-R3-69` through `ST3-MOMENT-R3-71`. All frames are web-first scale-in-context photographs in horizontal 3:2 format. No JSON, registry or unrelated report changes belong to this task.

## Product identity lock

- Each frame uses its matching accepted R2 product render as the sole authority for SKU identity.
- Preserve the R2 heavy optically clear flint-glass cylindrical vessel, short rounded shoulder, wide spoonable mouth, thick wall and visibly heavy base.
- When closed, preserve the flush thin graphite anodized-aluminum cap; visible cap height remains approximately 4–6% of total closed-vessel height.
- Preserve neutral-black direct print on glass. No paper label, sticker, sleeve, opaque backing or plastic package.
- Preserve the matching internal product trace: amber thyme-honey ribbon, warm-beige roasted-buckwheat body with sparse fine flecks, or thin olive-gold ribbon with restrained pearly interface.
- Exact visible SKU copy is limited to `MORA` plus the matching recipe name. No extra copy or pseudo-copy.

## Capture and web-role lock

- Primary web role: scale in context.
- Canvas: horizontal 3:2 landscape; delivered frames are 1536 × 1024 px.
- Viewpoint: natural eye-level, front-readable three-quarter placement.
- Distance: medium-wide; complete jar silhouette and a familiar scale cue share one surface plane at comparable camera distance.
- Occupancy: jar remains clearly dominant but contextual, approximately 45–55% of frame height in the accepted set.
- Lens behavior: natural perspective comparable to a restrained 50 mm commercial editorial photograph; no wide-angle distortion.
- Crop behavior: generous outer clearance and responsive tolerance; props do not overlap the direct print or silhouette.

## Light, color and realism lock

- One motivated soft upper-left key with restrained fill and gentle falloff.
- Quiet neutral-to-warm contemporary color response, moderate contrast, retained highlights and no crushed blacks.
- Controlled asymmetric glass reflections, plausible refraction, visible glass thickness, believable tabletop contact and soft grounded shadows.
- Moderate depth of field keeps both product and scale cue readable; no extreme bokeh or total-frame hyper-sharpness.
- Invisible retouching with natural surface variation. No CGI-like uniform gloss, HDR clarity, impossible reflection, floating vessel or duplicated object.

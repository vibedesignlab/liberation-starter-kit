## Landing role

Six 1:1 ingredient folio images for the existing `SplitEditorial` left-hand slots. These are factual raw-input references paired with existing Material Folio etchings, not mood scenes, product packshots, intermediate preparations, or origin/performance proof.

## Source authority

Accepted Stage 2 Revision 18 true-aerial raw ingredient images are the untouched native authorities:

- Thyme & Honey

## Slot lock

- exact 1:1 source and rendered ratio;
- strict 90-degree aerial axis;
- complete vessels remain visible under `object-fit: cover`;
- no product, yogurt, label, text, hand, prop, horizon, wall, or physical set beyond the stainless food-contact field;
- factual color and actual culinary scale;
- critical subject inside the central 76%, with the recorded exception that Fig Leaf retains approximately 10–11% horizontal margin rather than the target 12–14%.

## High-resolution delivery

The accepted 1254×1254 Stage 2 PNGs are preserved byte-for-byte in the Stage 3 native folder, then resampled to 2048×2048 PNG delivery masters. Resampling supplies the requested delivery dimensions but does not create or claim new ingredient detail. No aesthetic regeneration or second-pass styling review is performed.

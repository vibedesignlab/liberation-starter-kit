# Revision 8 — Recipe motion folios

## Shared direction

- Role: replace the four static recipe etchings inside the horizontal-scroll sequence with restrained motion folios.
- Format: square, 3 seconds, silent, seamless loop.
- Camera: fully locked. No zoom, pan, tilt, parallax or reframing.
- Continuity: preserve the original paper field, object count, object placement, scale, metal geometry, engraved linework and restrained warm-grey palette.
- Motion budget: one recipe-defining food action per folio. Everything else remains still.
- Loop construction: use the same registered etching as both first and last frame.

| Recipe | Single motion cue | Recipe meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thyme Honey | One viscous honey drop falls through the strainer; the existing yogurt fold settles by a few millimetres. | Infused honey is strained and folded, not drizzled on top. |
| Roasted Buckwheat | Three or four groats slip from the spoon into the yogurt and settle without a splash. | Toasted grain remains as an internal trace. |
| Citrus Peel | One fine peel thread relaxes from the grater toward the bowl; the existing furrow closes slightly. | Blanched peel is threaded through the body. |
| Black Sesame | The paste at the scraper tip stretches once and touches the bright yogurt; no stirring follows. | Dark sesame meets bright yogurt in one fold. |

## Negative constraints

No camera movement, hand movement, object drift, morphing, added or removed utensils, boiling, splashing, rapid stirring, ingredient shower, decorative garnish, new text, logo, watermark, CGI gloss, photoreal conversion or loss of etching linework.

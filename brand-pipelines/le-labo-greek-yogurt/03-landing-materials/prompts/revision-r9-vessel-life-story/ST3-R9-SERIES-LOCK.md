# Revision 9 — From final check to the table

## Narrative lock

The sequence is not a vessel instruction manual. It follows one Thyme Honey batch as responsibility passes from the female maker's final check in the workshop to another woman's active culinary judgment in a lived-in home. Neither woman is staged as a social cliché. Their authority is visible through choosing, reading, pairing and serving.

Sequence: `SEE / final approval` → `READ / responsibility handed over` → `OPEN / pairing decision` → `TASTE / internal fold meets food` → `TABLE / the work continues in use`.

## Product and label lock

- The same low-wide transparent MORA wide-mouth jar appears in every frame.
- The same Thyme Honey internal fold, glass proportions, rim, metal overcap or food seal state, partial uncoated paper record and label placement remain continuous.
- The partial Batch Record is operational evidence, never decoration. It is populated from the first frame and never reverts to blank lines.
- Required visible label hierarchy: `MORA`, `BATCH RECORD`, `RECIPE`, `THYME HONEY`, `MILK LOT`, `CULTURED`, `STRAINED`, `FOLDED`, `PACKED`, `KEEP COLD`, `NET WT`, `MAKER CHECK`.
- Display values for the concept image: `BATCH 01`, `CHECKED`, `ONE PASS`, `150 G`, maker initials `M.R.` and one restrained hand check. Do not invent a city, date, benefit, nutrition claim, signature flourish or legal certification.
- Food remains the largest readable surface. Paper covers no more than 30–40% of the visible sidewall.

## Photographic series lock

- Web role: full-bleed narrative feature sequence under the existing sticky bottom-left copy interaction.
- Native frame: exact 3:2 landscape. Delivery master: 6144×4096 PNG.
- Keep the bottom-left 40% width × bottom 28% height low-frequency and readable for white copy. Subjects, hands, props, strong reflections and hard shadows do not cross this zone.
- Viewpoint progression: environmental 50 mm → readable 65 mm → contextual 50 mm → food-detail 85 mm → lived-in 35–40 mm wide.
- Camera stays 100% front or restrained side/front editorial; no tilted fashion angle, macro-only ambiguity or oversized props.
- Real scale: 150 g jar, ordinary teaspoon, bread slice, pear, bowls and adult hands.
- Light progression: neutral grey workshop daylight → soft domestic daylight → warmer table practical. Every shadow and reflection follows a plausible visible or implied source.
- Capture response: natural analog magazine grain, gentle highlight roll-off, open shadow detail, slight density variation. No fake scratches, dust, light leaks, sepia or teal-orange grade.
- Preserve natural glass refraction, metal micro-use, food weight, cloth weave, skin texture and contact shadows. Avoid CGI gloss, plastic skin, perfect symmetry, cloned crumbs, floating utensils and studio-set cleanliness.

## Reference roles

1. `REF-LABEL`: strict-front filled Batch Record and jar geometry authority.
2. `REF-WORKSHOP`: real vintage workshop volume, stainless work surface, cloth, whey and final-check objects.
3. `REF-HOME`: lived-in kitchen volume and the household curator's working context.
4. `REF-PAIRING`: warm rye toast, roasted pear and thyme as the restrained Thyme Honey pairing palette.
